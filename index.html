04

Read the numbers carefully

Identification is not proof. A digest's length and encoding can narrow the possibilities, but they cannot always identify the algorithm with certainty. Salts and application-specific formats matter.

Time is a model, not a promise. The estimator uses approximate example rates and a half-keyspace average. It does not test, recover, or crack the submitted hash.

Use responsibly. Built for learning, defensive audits, and hashes you are authorized to examine. Never submit credentials or sensitive production data.
